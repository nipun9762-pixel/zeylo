Execution Pipeline

The Masterwork Process

01

Artwork & Digitizing

We manually translate your vector designs into precise stitch paths (DST/EMB formats), adjusting pull compensation, densities, and stitch direction angles.

02

Thread & Garment Matching

Pantone shade matching using high-tensile Madeira & Isacord threads across rayon silk, high-luster metallic, and fire-retardant poly variants.

03

Precision Stitching

Executed on multi-head Tajima embroidery machinery calibrated at up to 1,100 SPM with micro-tension monitoring to ensure zero puckering.

04

Quality Control & Delivery

Manual trimming of jump stitches, backing removal, steam pressing, and rigorous optical inspection before vacuum sealing and dispatch.
